LEAKY BUCKET ALGORITHM - Theory and Working
Computer Networks Laboratory

1. THEORY
The Leaky Bucket Algorithm is a traffic shaping mechanism used to
control the rate of data transmission across a computer network.

Key Principles:
- A bucket with a fixed capacity acts as a buffer.
- Incoming packets arrive at variable, bursty rates.
- Packets leak out (are transmitted) at a constant, fixed rate.
- If incoming packets exceed the remaining bucket buffer, the excess
  packets are discarded (dropped).
- The output traffic stream is always smooth and uniform.

2. WORKING (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Input the bucket capacity and constant output rate.
Step 2: Input the number of time intervals and packet sizes for each interval.
Step 3: At each tick:
  - Add incoming packets to current buffer.
  - If current buffer exceeds capacity, compute dropped packets.
  - Transmit packets up to the output rate.
  - Compute remaining buffer.
Step 4: Continue emptying the buffer at the fixed rate until zero.

4. VIVA QUESTIONS
Q1: What is the primary purpose of Leaky Bucket?
A1: To convert variable, bursty traffic into smooth, constant-rate output.

Q2: What happens when the bucket overflows?
A2: Excess packets that exceed bucket capacity are dropped.
